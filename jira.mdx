---
title: "Jira"
---

The Jira integration syncs issues from your Jira projects into Jinzo's entity store. Jira tickets become searchable locally and available as context in AI chat.

## **Authentication**

Jira uses **OAuth** for authentication. Jinzo will open a browser window to authorize access to your Atlassian account.

## **What Gets Synced**

| **Field**       | **Details**                                                               |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entity kind** | `issue`                                                                   |
| **Data synced** | Issue summary, description, status, priority, assignee, labels, issue key |

## **Setup**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Jira">
    Go to **Settings \> Apps** and click **Connect** next to Jira. Authorize Jinzo through the Atlassian OAuth flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Projects">
    Choose which Jira projects to sync. Only issues from selected projects will be fetched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial Sync">
    Jinzo fetches existing issues and then syncs incrementally on subsequent runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
