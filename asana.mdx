---
title: "Asana"
---

The Asana integration syncs tasks from your Asana projects into Jinzo's entity store. Tasks become searchable locally and available as context in AI chat.

## **Authentication**

Asana uses **OAuth** for authentication. Jinzo will open a browser window to authorize access to your Asana account.

## **What Gets Synced**

| **Field**       | **Details**                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entity kind** | `task`                                                        |
| **Data synced** | Task name, notes, completion status, assignee, due date, tags |

## **Setup**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Asana">
    Go to **Settings \> Apps** and click **Connect** next to Asana. Authorize Jinzo through the Asana OAuth flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Projects">
    Choose which Asana projects to sync. Only tasks from selected projects will be fetched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial Sync">
    Jinzo fetches existing tasks and then syncs incrementally on subsequent runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
