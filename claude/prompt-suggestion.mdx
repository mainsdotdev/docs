When the provider supplies a follow-up suggestion, Mains displays it near the conversation.

Clicking a suggestion **sends it as a follow-up** and starts the next turn. Read it before clicking. To change the request, write your own message in the composer.

## Availability

Claude, Codex, and Cursor can supply suggestions. Copilot currently does not expose this capability in Mains.

Suggestions depend on the provider and turn. An empty or missing suggestion is not a run failure.

## Use a suggestion

A suggestion may propose verifying a change, asking for an explanation, or taking the next step. Check whether the proposed action matches your goal before selecting it.

It starts an ordinary continuation and follows the same [context](/context-injection), settings, and [approval](/approvals) rules as your own message.
